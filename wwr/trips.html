TRIP DESCRIPTIONS

Devil's Fangs

Navigate a narrow gorge where jagged rocks frame the rushing river. This trip is for thrill-seekers who want to test their courage against sharp turns and powerful currents.

Big Mallard

A calmer journey through wide waters surrounded by lush green hills. Perfect for those who want to relax, enjoy nature, and paddle at a steady pace.

Half Day Adventure

Ideal for beginners or those short on time. Spend a few hours learning rafting basics, enjoying gentle waves, and soaking in the riverside atmosphere.

Devil's Cauldron

Dive into turbulent whitewater where swirling rapids challenge even experienced paddlers. This is a high-energy trip designed for adrenaline lovers.

Thunder Run

The ultimate test of teamwork and endurance. Intense rapids, steep drops, and constant splashes make this run unforgettable.

Every raft is accompanied by a certified guide, ensuring safety, navigation, and teamwork throughout the trip. Transportation to the launch site and return is also provided. The listed raft capacity refers to guests only, the guide is always included in addition to that number. Each adventure begins with a safety briefing and includes all equipment (helmets, life jackets, paddles, and rafts). Half-day trips run for four hours with a mid-trip snack break, while full-day trips last seven hours with a riverside lunch provided. Solo adventurers are welcome; if fewer guests arrive than the minimum, guides will join to balance the raft.

Group Discounts

At White Water Rafting Company, we love seeing teams, families, and friends take on the river together. To make it easier, we offer special group pricing:

  1. Groups of 4-6: Save 10% per person
  2. Groups of 7-10: Save 15% per person
  3. Groups of 11+: Save 20% per person + free group photo package

Important Booking Details:

  • Group discounts apply only when the trip is booked at least three days in advance.
  • Payment can be made in two ways:
    • Individually at the establishment when you arrive.
    • All together online at the time of booking.
  • To obtain an invoice, you must request it at reception or send an email to the company with your details.
